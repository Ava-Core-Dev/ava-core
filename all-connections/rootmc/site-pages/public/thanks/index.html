Redeem tutorial

  1. Hold an Appreciation Token (sunflower) in your main hand.
  2. Make sure you have at least one empty inventory slot. Hotbar, backpack, or offhand / shield slot all count.
  3. Right-click air (or a non-shop block). The server spends 1 token and rolls 1 reward from the catalog. Right-clicking a shop sign or chest trades instead of redeeming.
  4. You get a personal win message, and the whole server sees: who redeemed and what they won.
  5. Optional: /thanks redeem (or /thanks redeem 3 to roll three times if you have space and tokens).

Free Lotto Ticket wins grant a Root-Gamble /lotto free-play credit (same as your first unused free ticket) — not a numbered slip.

How to earn

  • Voting — each confirmed vote grants 1 Appreciation Token, 1 digital Vote Shard (same number as listing votes; physical /ec items unused), and 1–20 G. 5 votes in 24 hours halves tax and death-loss. In-game /vote shows the full note (totals, timers, perk, Council share). Tokens redeem loot; shards only vote. Extra shards: buy Vote Shards ($1 = 100, min $0.50).
  • /bonus — daily streak (tokens + Gold from the Server Reserve).
  • Staff events / memberships may also issue tokens.

Tokens are inventory items with tracking lore. You can redeem them, trade them in player shops, or keep them.

Trading

  • Create a chest shop while holding a token — the listing key is APPRECIATION_TOKEN (not plain sunflower).
  • Stock tokens in the shop chest, or deposit via /item and list from virtual storage.
  • Buyers find them in /market or by right-clicking your shop sign.
  • Plain sunflowers are a different item and will not fulfill an Appreciation Token shop.

/thanks stats

/thanks (aliases /appreciation, /apptoken) shows global and personal issued / redeemed totals, circulating supply, inventory count, listing-vote token totals, digital Vote Shard note, and your next /bonus hint. Run /vote for the full vote note (sites, gold, shards, Council power).

FAQ

Why was my redeem blocked?

You need one empty slot (including offhand). Armor slots do not count. Clear a backpack or offhand slot and try again.

Can I sell tokens in shops?

Yes — player chest shops and virtual listings. The shop item is APPRECIATION_TOKEN. Right-click a shop while holding a token to trade; right-click air to redeem.

Do old tokens still work?

Yes — PDC marks the item. Lore refreshes on join after the redeem update.

Where does bonus Gold come from?

The Server Reserve — see the economy guide.
