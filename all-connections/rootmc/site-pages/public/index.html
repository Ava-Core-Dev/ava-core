About the network

Official play, shared tools

The RootMC Network centers on our official primary survival world — long-form play built around cooperation, trade, and territory — not pay-to-win. Join at play.rootmc.net.

What makes official RootMC different

  • Closed-loop economy — Gold (G) from minting, shops, and trade. Rules in the Constitution; how-to in the economy guide · live data (redesign in progress).
  • Claims & land — Personal Root-Claims circles, territory buffers, and ranks on the primary world.
  • McMMO progression — Classic skill grinding with public stats once you link your account.
  • Discord bridge — In-game chat mirrors to Discord; community votes and weekly awards keep both sides connected.

Fair play & linking

We expect respect, no cheating (x-ray, dupes, unfair clients), and honest trade. Linking your Minecraft account unlocks the linked-player Discord role, public stats pages, and the RootMC Android companion app. Rules: Constitution · commands: player wiki.

Weekly recognition

Every Sunday at 08:00 HST we post Top Participator (Discord messages, votes, and reactions) and Top Active Player (linked in-game playtime). Roles reset each week — when awards are posted, the leaders appear in Discord and on this page.
