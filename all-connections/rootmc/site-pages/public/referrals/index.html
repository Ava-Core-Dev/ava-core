How it works

  1. Get a code — run /root code in-game. You get a short unique code (6 characters).
  2. Share it — send the code to a friend who is new (or newly joining) RootMC.
  3. They claim — within 48 hours of first join, they run /root claim YOURCODE.
  4. They link — both of you need Discord linked via /discord / rootmc.net/verify.
  5. They play — after about 3 hours of playtime (Root-Times), the referral qualifies.
  6. You get paid — 75 G from the Server Reserve hits your wallet (loan sweep may apply).

Until they qualify, the branch is pending. Pending does not pay Gold.

Qualification gates

A referral only becomes permanent (and pays) when all of these pass:

  • Recruit claimed a valid code (not their own)
  • Claim within 48 hours of first join (configurable)
  • Recruit has Discord linked to Minecraft (1:1 — one Discord per UUID)
  • Referrer is Discord-linked (required by default)
  • Recruit has at least 3 hours playtime tracked by Root-Times

Same Discord cannot farm multiple Minecraft accounts for referrals. Soft same-IP flags go to staff review — they do not auto-ban.

Rewards (closed economy)

All referral Gold is debited from the Server Reserve — the same closed-loop treasury used for Discord link bonuses, Root-Try, and other grants. Nothing is minted from thin air.

Milestones

Extra one-time grants when your qualified recruit count hits thresholds (still under the lifetime cap):

  • 5 qualified → +50 G
  • 15 qualified → +100 G
  • 30 qualified → +200 G

Fair play

  • Depth is 1 for now — you earn from people you personally referred, not from their recruits.
  • No tax-share residual — we do not skim a percent of a recruit’s taxes forever.
  • Alts that share one Discord will not qualify as separate branches.
  • Staff can inspect trees, unlink edges, and purge abuse cases.

FAQ

Can I claim a code after playing a while?

Only within the claim window (default 48 hours from first join). After that, ask staff only if there was a clear mistake.

I claimed but my friend got nothing

Check /root info — if status is pending, finish Discord link and playtime. Use /root tree to see recruit status.

Does this work on live RootMC?

Yes. Edges live in shared MySQL on play.rootmc.net, so a recruit credits a referrer when gates pass.

Where do I download the plugin?

Suite jar: rootmc.net/plugins (Root-Referrals) once published on the manifest.
