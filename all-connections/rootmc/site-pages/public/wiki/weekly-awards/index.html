When awards post

Awards run every Sunday at 08:00 HST (Pacific/Honolulu) for the previous Mon–Sun week.

  • Winners are announced in RootMC Discord general chat.
  • Last week’s role holders lose the award role; this week’s winners receive it fresh.
  • Recent winners also appear on the rootmc.net home page after the post goes live.

Top Participator (Discord)

The Top Participator role goes to the top 5 Discord members who contributed the most meaningful activity that week.

Staff are included. #ingame-chat bridge messages count. Automated bot channels (e.g. #bot-spam, daily report feeds) are not counted toward messages or reactions.

You need at least 5 weighted points in a week to qualify.

How Discord points work

ActionPoints
Message block — one run of your messages until someone else speaks in that channel1
Community vote — /proposal vote (linked players)5 each
Reaction — emoji on a community message (bots excluded)1 each

Weighted score = blocks + (votes × 5) + reactions.

Example: 3 message blocks, 2 votes, and 4 reactions = 3 + 10 + 4 = 17 points.

Message blocks (not every message)

Back-to-back messages from you in the same channel count as one block — not one point per message.

A new block starts when you post again after another person has spoken in that channel. Rapid “e e e e” floods in a row without anyone else talking are still one block and one point.

Activity is synced from Discord into the database on a schedule. Votes are recorded when you cast them on proposals.

Root-AI judgment

After raw scores are calculated, Root-AI reviews the week’s saved activity for top candidates and:

  • Ranks the final top 5 Top Participators.
  • Excludes spam — letter floods, filler-only bursts, and low-effort noise can be removed from that week’s calculations even if the raw score looked high.

If Root-AI is unavailable, the server falls back to weighted scores with automatic spam heuristics.

Gaming the counter with meaningless spam won’t help — you still get judged by Root-AI.

Top Active Players (in-game)

The Top Active Player Discord role and in-game [Top Active Player] prefix go to the top 3 linked players by playtime earned that week only — not lifetime playtime.

  • Playtime comes from RootStat sync totals (end-of-week snapshot minus the prior Sunday snapshot).
  • You must be linked — Minecraft account tied to Discord via /link and web verify.
  • Minimum 1 hour of in-game time that week to qualify.

The first week after playtime tracking starts may only seed a baseline; awards begin once a full week of deltas exists.

Pro membership reward

Only the #1 Top Active Player receives one week of Pro membership:

  • App — higher AI report limits and other RootMC app perks on the linked account.
  • In-game — chat shows a [Pro] prefix (synced on join from your linked account), in addition to [Top Active Player] for ranks 1–3.
  • Granted via your linked account email — sign into the app with the same account you verified in-game.
  • Life members and paid Pro are not overwritten.
  • Award Pro does not grant Vote Shards or the Council ×2 multiplier — cosmetics + app only.
  • Next Sunday: if you are not #1 again, award Pro is removed (paid / life Pro stays). Top-3 prefix/role refresh with the new week’s winners.

Discord Top Participator winners receive the Discord role only — not automatic Pro (unless you also place #1 in-game).

Paid Pro & Lifetime

Separate from weekly awards, you can support the server with a RootMC Pro Membership. Full perk list and checkout buttons: rootmc.net/pro. At checkout, enter the Minecraft username who should receive Pro (yourself or a gift).

  • $4.99 / month — subscription Pro on that linked account while the sub is active (not an item).
  • $4.99 one-time — one-month Pro voucher delivered to that player’s /vault; tradeable until redeemed in-game.
  • $75 one-time — Lifetime Pro voucher the same way; redeem for permanent Lifetime.

The beneficiary must already have a linked Minecraft account. Weekly award Pro is temporary and separate from paid months and Lifetime. Paid Pro grants 500 Vote Shards / month + ×2; Lifetime grants 500 / month for life + ×3 + listing-site ×2. Buy extra shards: rootmc.net/pro/vote-shards ($1 = 100, min $0.50). See Vote Shards.

FAQ

Do I need to link to compete on Discord?

No for Top Participator — Discord activity counts from your Discord user ID. Linking is required for in-game awards and Pro membership.

Why didn’t my spam messages rank me?

Root-AI excludes obvious spam from that week. One-word floods in a row are only one message block anyway.

Does staff activity count?

Yes on Discord. In-game playtime uses the same rules for everyone with a linked account.

When does my Pro week start?

After the Sunday award cron runs — only the #1 Top Active Player gets weekly Pro. Check the app account tied to your linked email.
