Overview

RootMC’s economy is closed-loop: new players start at 0 G, earn through play and trade, and every automated grant is paid from the Server Reserve (Server-Reserve). Authoritative rates and grant policy: Constitution.

RootMC’s economy is built on Root-Economy (Vault balances in MySQL), player chest shops, Root-Claims (land and claim-bank costs), and personal/town loans. Root-Essentials keeps QoL commands and sinks fees into the treasury SPI. The RootMC plugin syncs balances, shops, treasury data, and playtime to the cloud for the stock market, leaderboards, and your public stats page.

Design principles:

  • No money faucet — starting balance is 0 G. New gold enters when players mint ore, sell items, or receive transfers from other players.
  • Closed-loop treasury — taxes, fees, loan repayments, and death fees flow into the Server Reserve; grants, loans, votes, milestones, bond payments, and refunds flow out.
  • Onboarding from the reserve — playtime milestone rewards pay to your wallet but debit the Server Reserve (same pool as loans and votes).
  • Player-priced shops — the optional rolling-average price ceiling is currently disabled. Use /shop avg <item> to compare prices.
RootMC Constitution is the authoritative source for all rules, reserve policy, taxes, and rates. Live totals and charts: economy page. Daily AI economy briefs post in Discord at midnight HST. This guide explains how to play the economy.

RootMC has four spendable account groups. Other records track obligations or queued payments but are not extra wallets.

Player ↔ Player: /pay, shops, contracts Player or bank → Reserve: taxes, fees, hire, rank and claim charges, loan repayment, bonds · /pay Ava_Ivy = /pay reserve Reserve → Player or bank: grants, votes, milestones, loans, bond payments, unclaim refunds

Every treasury movement is recorded in the reserve ledger. Mint backing is physical gold only at the fixed peg of 9 G/block, 1 G/ingot, and ¹⁄₉ G/nugget. Raw gold is not mintable — smelt it first. Server Reserve on /reserve is mint backing minus circulating Notes (negative when over-issued).

What is G?

G is your spendable balance — shown in chat, shops, claims, and on the web as gold. It is stored in the server economy database (not items in your inventory until you mint or buy).

Physical gold → balance (minting)

ItemValue when minted
Gold nugget¹⁄₉ G each (~0.11 G)
Gold ingot1 G each
Gold block9 G each

Use /mint hand (held stack) or /mint all (all gold items in inventory). Minting consumes the items and credits your balance.

/sell hand, /sell all, and /sell blocks sell non-gold items using live shop averages when available, otherwise the server worth table (e.g. diamond, iron, cobblestone defaults in config).

Earning G

  • Mine & mint — gold ore → wallet Notes (site multipliers) and mint backing. Smelt leftover raw, then /mint hand|all. Redeem Notes with /mint gold.
  • Sell items — /sell commands price against market averages or the worth table.
  • Player shops — stock a rootmc-shops chest; buyers pay your balance when they purchase. See Player shops.
  • Services & trade — /pay, player contracts (/contract), barter, jobs. Other players are the source of funds.
  • Playtime milestones — small gold grants at logged playtime tiers; paid to your wallet from the Server Reserve (see below).
  • Vote sites — /vote links; 1–20 G per confirmed vote plus Appreciation Tokens and Vote Shard weight (Vote rewards). Extra shards: buy.
  • Daily /bonus — Appreciation Token streak (Day 1–5) also pays 5–25 G from the Server Reserve; see Appreciation Tokens.
  • PvP — in player-vs-player kills, you may receive a share of the victim’s death fee (see Death fee recycling).

Votes pay you directly from the Server Reserve. Taxes, death fees, and loan repayments refill the reserve over time.

Spending G

  • Player shops — right-click a shop chest or /buy <item> [qty] (quote + confirm; cheapest in-stock listing, up to 2304).
  • Service fees — /survey, /shop stock, /warp create, /rank buy (see Service fees).
  • Claims — founding areas, expanding circles, claim-bank deposits. See Claims & land costs.
  • Pay players — /pay <player> <amount> (you send the full amount; the recipient receives it minus the live transaction tax tier).
  • Loan repayment — while in debt, incoming income pays the loan first (Root-Loans).
  • Contracts — escrow held until work is approved (/contract offer).

There is no NPC server shop — all item purchases come from other players’ listings.

Server Reserve treasury

Full reserve rules: Constitution — Server Reserve · inflows · outflows

RootMC maintains a hidden server treasury account (Server-Reserve / Ava_Ivy) that collects gold from economic activity. Ava’s /bal is this vault — /pay Ava and hire fees land here. It is the Server Reserve — shown on /baltop (no args) and in full on /reserve. It does not appear on player wallet leaderboards. Staff can audit the ledger; players see summary stats in-game with /reserve and on their public profile after linking.

Percentage taxes & withholdings

Transaction tax — reserve ledger tiers

The payer or buyer is debited the quoted gross. Tax is withheld from that amount, and the recipient or seller receives the net. Tax normally credits the reserve; while Notes are over-issued and the reserve ledger is non-negative, it is burned instead.

July 1+ reserve ledgerRateExample (100 G transfer)
≥ 1,000 G0.05% minRecipient gets 99.95 G; reserve 0.05 G
< 1,000 G1%Recipient gets 99 G; reserve 1 G
< 0 G (current era)2%Recipient gets 98 G; reserve 2 G
< −1,000 G3%Recipient gets 97 G; reserve 3 G
< −2,000 G4%Recipient gets 96 G; reserve 4 G
< −4,000 G5%Recipient gets 95 G; reserve 5 G
< −10,000 G10%Recipient gets 90 G; reserve 10 G

Live rate: /tax in-game or Ava economy board. Base config transaction-tax-rate: 0.001 applies only when dynamic tax is off. Wallet /mint backing is separate from reserve-ledger tiers. A 0.05% floor always applies. Offline solar adds +1% env (solar bonus). First claim plot is tax-free; later plots and expansions use this reserve tax. 5 listing votes in 24 hours halves your tax (and death loss) while that window holds — /vote.

You are never charged tax twice on the same transfer. If you have an active loan, income sweeps apply to your net receipt after tax where configured. Details: Transaction tax, Personal loans.

Death fee — 15% of wallet

The fee applies to all deaths, has a 1 G minimum, and never exceeds the wallet balance. With 5 listing votes in the last 24 hours, both the percent and the minimum are halved (7.5% / 0.5 G) until the rolling window drops below 5.

SplitShareOn 1,000 G victim

→ Server Reserve (PvP)40% of fee60 G

On mob, fall, lava, and other environmental deaths, the entire 150 G example fee goes to the reserve.

Shop price guidance

The optional 110% rolling-average ceiling is currently disabled. Players set prices; use /shop avg <item> to compare the market.

What is not taxed by the server

  • First claim plot — founding the first circle is tax-free
  • Playtime milestones and vote rewards — these pay out from the reserve, not into it
  • Staff grants — treasury outflow, not player tax
  • Creating a shop — free; only trades and bulk stock incur fees
  • Mining below Y 0 — free-reign underworld; no claim or wilderness destroy fees
Quick check in-game: /reserve shows your month-to-date transaction tax. /loan info shows sweep status. Claim costs appear in /c confirm prompts before you pay.

Transaction tax (reserve ledger tiers)

Every taxed transfer has a 0.05% minimum, then the reserve tier (backing − circulating Notes) if higher, then +1% env when solar is offline. It applies to:

  • /pay <player> <amount>
  • Player shop purchases (/buy and chest clicks)
  • Player shop sales to buy-shops (/sell)
  • /mint wallet credit

The payer or buyer always pays the quoted amount only. Tax is withheld from the receiver’s side and credited to the Server Reserve. Loan income sweeps still apply to the net amount you receive where configured.

Example at the current tier (reserve below 0 G, 2%): you buy from a shop for 100 G — you pay 100 G; the seller receives 98 G and the reserve gets 2 G. With the vote perk active that tax is halved. Check /tax for today's rate.

Death fee recycling

RootMC uses a custom death fee on every death. There is no separate land-plugin death charge.

RuleValue
Applies whenEvery death (PvP and environmental)
Fee size15% of wallet; minimum 1 G, capped at available balance. ½ with 5 votes / 24h
Environmental / mob death100% of fee → Server Reserve
PvP treasury share40% of fee → Server Reserve
PvP killer share60% of fee → killer’s balance

Example: victim has 1,000 G → fee 150 G. Environmental death: reserve receives 150 G. PvP: reserve receives 60 G, killer receives 90 G.

Vote rewards

Run /vote in-game for links to listing sites. When a site confirms your vote (via Votifier), the server rolls 1–20 G and pays it to your wallet from the Server Reserve (not minted).

The same confirmed vote also grants Appreciation Tokens and +1 digital EC shard (same number as your listing vote count). Lifetime listing sites count ×2. Paid shards ($1 = 100, min $0.50) are separate — vote value only, not Gold. Buy: rootmc.net/pro/vote-shards. Explainer: Vote Shards.

Streak perk: 5 confirmed listing votes in the last 24 hours halves your transaction tax and death-loss until that rolling window drops below 5. /vote and /tax show progress. Hitting 5 broadcasts in-game and Discord.

Chat message: “…paid from the Server Reserve.” Each listing site enforces its own cooldown; RootMC does not add an extra plugin cooldown.

Vote sites include Minecraft-MP, MinecraftServers.org, Planet Minecraft, MineRank, TopMinecraftServers, and others (see /vote output).

Player shops & market

Root-ChestShops holds stock in chest shops and optional virtual bins (/item deposit/withdraw, My Storage GUI). Root-Market (/market) is the in-game browser — categories, live buy/sell quotes from player shops, trending sell multi — not a separate warehouse. Listings also sync to the web stock market every ~5 minutes when at least one player is online.

Quick reference

  • Create — chest/barrel + hold item + /shop create or /shop create <price> (optional price sets total G in one step; otherwise type price in chat).
  • Buy — right-click a sell-shop sign (that shop only) or /buy <item> [amount] (equal split across same-price listings; confirm before charge).
  • Sell to buy shops — /sell <item> [amount] (equal split at the best price; sign click = that buy shop only).
  • Bulk stock — /shop stock → confirm 5 G merchant fee (only charged when items are actually stocked).
  • Price comparison — use /shop avg <item>; the optional 110% ceiling is currently disabled.
  • Appreciation Tokens — list as APPRECIATION_TOKEN (not plain sunflower). Hold a token + /shop create. Right-clicking a token on a shop sign/chest buys/sells — it does not redeem.

Full step-by-step with sign colors, buy shops, and legacy QuickShop notes: Opening a player shop in the player guide.

Fair split at the same price

/buy and /sell use the lowest (or highest for sell-to-buy-shop) in-stock price tier. Every shop at that exact price shares the order equally — 64 items across 4 shops is 16 each. One item among ties goes to a random shop. Right-clicking a shop sign always trades with that shop only. Five or more shops in one quote show a summary line (“Buying 64 IRON_INGOT from 5 players”) instead of listing every owner.

Shop trades: the customer pays the listed price; the live transaction tax tier is withheld from the seller’s or seller-side payout to the treasury.

Inactivity tax (Root-Upkeep)

Root-Upkeep runs once per Minecraft day (~30 real minutes). It debits a small percentage from idle balances into the Server Reserve — the same closed-loop treasury as transaction tax and claim sinks. Each charge is broadcast server-wide in chat.

Who gets taxed

TargetWhen tax applies
Player walletYou have not logged in for ≥ 30 real days
Claim bankNo owner or trusted member has logged in within 30 real days

If any trusted member of a claim has been active within the grace window, that claim bank is skipped — one active member keeps the area alive.

Tax rate by idle week

After the 30-day grace period, the rate applied each MC day scales sharply with total idle time:

Idle weekDaily rate (% of current balance)
Days 30–361%
Days 37–435%
Days 44–5010%
Day 51+100%

Minimum balance and minimum tax are 0.01 G. Logging back in resets your personal idle clock. For claims, any trusted-member login resets the claim clock.

Inactivity tax is not minting — gold moves from idle wallets and banks into the Server Reserve for redistribution (loans, votes, grants). Stay active or check in occasionally to avoid slow balance erosion on abandoned accounts.

Personal loans

Root-Loans offers optional credit after a community vote — borrow G for startup costs (e.g. a second claim area) when you are short on gold.

  • Default borrow limit 100 G; with purchased ranks, cap equals that tier’s /rank buy price (up to 150,000 G at Champion). Staff use the same rule — only player-track ranks count.
  • 10% interest at borrow, then 1% daily on the amount still owed (minimum accrual 0.01 G)
  • While in debt, 100% of incoming gold pays the loan first (including mint/sell/shop income)
  • Disbursements draw from the Server Reserve — if the pool is empty, /loan take fails until inflows return

Full rules, commands, and gold-ore behavior while in debt: Personal loans in the player guide.

Net worth & tracking

Net worth ≈ Vault balance + valued inventory + redeemable gold in player chests + shop stock + physical gold in storage at the mint peg (see Constitution — Net worth). Sync exports all player wallets from MySQL — claim banks are not counted as player balance on web leaderboards.

Shop stock counts quantity in your sell chests, valued at the same blended buy-side reference used on stock market charts — median in-stock sell average blended with recent history — not your individual listing prices.

FAQ

Does the server give free starter money?

No lump-sum grant. You start at 0 G and earn via minting, selling, milestones, votes, and trade.

Why didn’t I get gold from voting?

Vote gold lands in your wallet when the listing confirms via Votifier. It is debited from the Server Reserve, not minted. If it is missing, confirm the listing accepted the vote and that your Minecraft name matches.

Can the Server Reserve go negative?

Yes. Grants, milestones, and vote rewards debit the reserve ledger even when inflows lag. A negative balance on /reserve means outflows exceeded inflows — not that the server printed free gold. Taxes, claim charges, loan repayments, bond issues, donations, and service fees refill it over time.

Do I pay tax when someone pays me?

The sender or buyer is debited the quoted gross. Tax is withheld from that gross, so the recipient or seller receives the net. There is no second charge after receipt.

Why was I charged on death?

All deaths charge 15% of wallet balance (minimum 1 G, capped at the wallet). Environmental deaths send the full fee to reserve; PvP splits it 40% reserve / 60% killer.

Can I compare claim tax rates before I settle?

Claim tax on extra plots follows the live server reserve tier — the same rate for everyone. Compare land on the web (claims guide) or ask in Discord.

What is inactivity tax?

After 30 real days without logging in, your wallet is charged 1% per MC day, rising to 5%, 10%, then 100% as inactivity continues. Claim banks are protected when any trusted member is active within the grace window. See Inactivity tax.

Where do claim founding fees go?

Your first plot is free. Extra areas and expansions debit wallets or claim banks and credit the Server Reserve — they are not destroyed. See Claims & areas.

Where can I see every tax and fee rate?

The Constitution — Taxes & fees lists all percentage taxes, flat fees, claim costs, loan charges, and exemptions. This guide summarizes gameplay.
