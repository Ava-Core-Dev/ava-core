Open /bonds in-game to issue Gold Backed Bond certificates, collect coupon gold, or redeem principal. Every bond is backed by legit gold found on the map and stored via /mint - the same pool that covers all circulating claims. Live stats below sync from the server.

What are Gold Backed Bonds?

Gold Backed Bonds are named paper certificates tied directly to stored map gold in the /mint ledger and the Server Reserve. When you issue a bond, wallet Notes debit into the reserve and you receive a certificate you can store (an empty ender chest works well).

Safe savings for citizens

Town and nation banks are for mayors and kings - ordinary citizens cannot deposit into them. Bonds give every player a secure way to move surplus Gold out of a hot wallet and into Gold Backed Bond custody, without giving up ownership. Your principal stays yours on paper until you redeem it.

Always redeemable to gold

Gold Backed Bonds are not abstract credit. Hold the certificate, open /bonds, and redeem - principal pays out as physical gold from stored /mint gold at the same peg as everywhere else on RootMC. Stored gold vs circulating claims is tracked on the economy overview; any surplus stored gold shows as Missing (unaccounted until it circulates).

Income from the reserve

Each Minecraft day (~30 minutes), bond holders share 25% of that day's Server Reserve inflows (taxes, fees, loan repayments, and similar), split pro-rata by outstanding principal. Active holders receive earnings in their wallet automatically (registered note owner). After 28 days without logging in, bond notes are auto-redeemed and inactivity tax applies.

Why it matters

Gold Backed Bonds link personal savings to stored map gold and the public treasury: your G helps fund the reserve, you earn when the server earns, and this page shows who holds bonds and how coupons settle. It is the citizen-facing counterpart to town auto-bonds - transparent, redeemable, and synced here on rootmc.net.
