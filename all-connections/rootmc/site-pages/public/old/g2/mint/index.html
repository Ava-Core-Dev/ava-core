How it works

The mint is a conversion function, not a vault

Loading...

/mint destroys physical gold items and credits wallet Notes (G) at a fixed peg, or burns Notes and spawns gold via /mint gold. It does not maintain its own account or gold reserve - only an audited treasury ledger of every conversion. The Server Reserve (towny-server) is separate: governance grants and treasury flows, not mint holdings.

Fixed peg (1 Note = 1 gold ingot)

  • Gold nugget - 1/9 G
  • Gold ingot - 1 G
  • Gold block - 9 G
