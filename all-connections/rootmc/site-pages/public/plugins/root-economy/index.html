Root-Economy

Owns the RootMC Gold economy — wallets, claim banks, Vault High registration, treasury SPI, shops, compounding bonds, loans, Server Reserve, and MC-day heartbeat. Root-Essentials stays QoL/travel and debits fees through this plugin.

What it includes

  • Vault Economy — High priority so other plugins see RootMC Gold
  • Wallets — /bal, /pay, /baltop, /gold, /mint, /reserve, /economy, /tax, /grant. Ava_Ivy is Server-Reserve; /pay Ava = /pay reserve (no tax). Hire and other plugin fees sink here.
  • Claim banks — area banks on Root-Claims (/c bank); not town or nation banks
  • Shops — chest shops (/shop, /buy, /sell)
  • Bonds — park Gold; MC-day yield compounds into principal; /bonds
  • Loans — personal loans from the Server Reserve
  • Upkeep — inactivity tax only (no town-bank daily tax). Claim tax: first area free; later areas + expansions use reserve tax (/tax)
  • Solar bonus — disconnected: gold + skills 1.00× and +1% env tax. Connected: 1 + 1% + 1% per 10% battery + 1% per 100 W on gold and skills (example 50% bank + 500 W → 1.11×). No CPU tax. Transaction tax always has a 0.05% floor, then reserve tier. 5 listing votes / 24h halves that tax and death-loss. Site taxes on mine drops: mesa 50%, nether 25%, Y −20–20 +1%; deepslate gold 1.5×. /radar 4-block NESW dash tape + ore pips (1 G/min). Gold ore → Notes + mint backing. Live: /gold, /tax, /mint, /reserve, /vote

Install order

  1. Root-Core → Root-Times → Root-Perms
  2. Root-Economy (this jar) — load before other economy consumers
  3. Root-Essentials — soft-depends Root-Economy for fee sinks
  4. Root-Claims — live land + claim banks

After upgrade, remove older root-economy-*.jar and retired standalone Bonds/Loans/Shops/Upkeep jars.
