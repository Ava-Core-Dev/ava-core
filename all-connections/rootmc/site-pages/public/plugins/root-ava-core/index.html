Root-Ava-Core

Ava’s Paper companion — in-game chat, solar/host status, hire hour/sidekick/dig, and operator hooks. Start with /ava help for a short how-to. Paid work orders resume after disconnect or restart. Hire fees sink to the Server Reserve (Ava’s /bal is that vault). Root-Core ensure-installs this jar from manifest.json even when only-installed: true.

In-game

  • /ava help — how to hire (stand in claim → pick a job)
  • /ava hire hour|sidekick|mine|teardown — start immediately (also /ava hour, /ava minedown, …). Dig jobs stand at the work blocks (do not follow). Sidekick still follows.
  • /ava hire — your land only (own claim or territory). Not trusted, not others, not wilderness
  • /ava hire claim / /ava hire minedown — quote, then click [Confirm]. Claim clear: full claim circle (center or rim, including your expansions) from stand Y, two rows at a time top down (rescans the pair for grass/skips before going down). Natural blocks only — player builds are left standing. Quote shows ores at Y -59, ETA, and builds skipped. Stop via /ava stop · no refunds. No base fee: 0.01 G/block, +0.01 per 100k. Dig max 480/min; skips the floor under players
  • /ava stop — work-order list with red [Stop]. /ava hire status for ETA. Paid jobs resume after disconnect or restart
  • Right-click Ava (body or Ava_Ivy) — menu: free bread + your current hire job / quote
  • /ava gift · /ava work · /ava eco — chests, own-claim work, Server Reserve

Self-install

  1. Install Root-Core and keep updater.enabled: true.
  2. Leave updater.ensure including root-ava-core (default), or set only-installed: false to pull the full catalog.
  3. Run /rootcore update or wait for the scheduled poll. Restart Paper to load the new jar.
