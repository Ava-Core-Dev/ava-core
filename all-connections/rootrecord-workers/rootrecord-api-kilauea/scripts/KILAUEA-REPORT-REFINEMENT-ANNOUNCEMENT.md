**Kīlauea Alerts — smarter hazard briefs**

We refined how AI hazard reports are built so they stay focused on what matters for Hawaiʻi Island.

**What changed**
• Reports now scope to **Kīlauea, Hawaiʻi Island, and Hawaiʻi-relevant hazards** — not routine mainland volcano roundups.
• Official social feeds (including @USGSVolcanoes) are **filtered before** the brief is written. Routine observatory updates from other regions — for example Cascades “normal background” weeklies — are dropped unless they are **massive, life-threatening, or tsunami-related** for Pacific / Hawaiʻi interests.
• AI instructions were tightened so off-topic mainland activity is not repeated in public briefs.

**What stays the same**
• USGS/HVO volcano notices, NWS Hawaiʻi alerts, Big Island earthquake counts, and tsunami products when relevant.
• `/kilauea` manual reports and automatic cron briefs use the same pipeline.

**Why**
A recent brief included a routine Cascades Volcano Observatory summary that was unrelated to Kīlauea. That noise is now filtered at the source and in the AI prompt.
