What Root Units are

Roots are account credits stored on your RootRecord account ledger. They are used by Roots Idle Farmer and related RootRecord community features. Your balance follows your signed-in account, so the same total appears here, on Account, in Root Farms, and in Discord after linking.

How to buy Roots

Open Roots Idle Farmer, sign in, then open the Orchards/Treasury section. Use Buy internal ROOTS with SOL to preview a quote and purchase. The current internal rate is shown in the game before you buy, and SOL moves to the RootRecord treasury while internal Roots are credited to your account.

If you already hold the on-chain ROOTS token, the game can show your custodial wallet address. Sending supported ROOTS deposits to that custodial wallet lets the processor sweep the token to treasury and credit your internal Roots balance after confirmation.

How to earn Roots

Participating RootRecord apps may credit Roots while you are signed in and using the app in good faith. The system measures session activity and may apply per-screen or daily limits so earning stays fair. Daily earning limits can vary by app and may change as the program evolves.

How to use Roots

In Roots Idle Farmer: Roots are your in-game balance for farm purchases, market actions, and progression. The game is available at farms.rootrecord.info.

On Discord: after you link Discord, use /bal to check your balance and /send to send Roots to another linked member.

On the web: use this page for your balance and guide, Account for account details, and Root Economy for public circulation charts.

Troubleshooting

If your balance does not appear, sign in again from Account and return to this page. If a purchase or deposit is pending, wait for Solana confirmation and refresh the game or this page. If Discord commands do not work, unlink and relink Discord from your Account page.

Not financial advice

Root Units are an experimental loyalty, game, and usage feature. They are non-cash account credits and are not a guarantee of any future payment or monetary value. Program rules, rates, app eligibility, and availability may change or end at any time, subject to the Terms and Privacy.
