Principles in practice

Why people choose RootRecord.

●

On your devices

Business Manager on Android keeps your signed-in workspace with RootRecord’s API; export and backups follow the paths documented in the app. Weather Manager keeps working data on your Android device unless you enable features that sync online. Kilauea Alerts keeps dashboards and alerts on your device, with optional summaries from RootRecord’s API where enabled.

●

Works in the real world

Day-to-day work is not blocked when the network is spotty. Sign-in, sync, and cloud backup layer on when you enable them.

●

Honest totals

Hours worked, categories, exports — numbers reconcile by design. No surprises when it's time for reports and invoices.

●

Subscription & online

Subscription, sync, and secure online backup when you want them. One account across RootRecord apps where supported.

●

Google Play & updates

Business Manager, Weather Manager, and Kīlauea Alerts install from Google Play like public apps. Other Android builds may use closed testing or GitHub Releases; each app shows its version in About.

●

Lifetime option

One payment for ongoing access to current and future RootRecord programs — so the tool grows with your work.
