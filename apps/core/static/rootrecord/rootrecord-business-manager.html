Products / Business Manager

RootRecord Business Manager.

Time, money, clients, stock, scheduling, work log, and reports — one capable workspace on Android (Google Play) and the web at business.rootrecord.info, with your RootRecord account.

Web — open in any browser

Use the full Business Manager from any desktop or laptop browser at business.rootrecord.info. Same RootRecord account as the Android app; your workspace, clients, and time entries stay in sync.

Open business.rootrecord.info Pricing

No install required — sign in with your RootRecord account.

Android — Google Play

RootRecord Business Manager is public on Google Play. Open the listing on your Android phone or tablet, tap Install, and sign in inside the app with your RootRecord account. Updates arrive through the Play Store like any other app.

What you get

Everything you need, nothing more.

●

Time & work log

Track time with sensible totals — overlapping clock intervals merge so minutes are never double-counted.

●

Money & clients

Invoices, categories, projects, tags — clear books you can stand behind at tax time or on a client call.

●

Inventory & supplies

Track stock, supplies, and consumables alongside your work. Units, costs, and counts in one place.

●

Schedule

A real schedule view for appointments, jobs, and recurring work — linked to clients and time entries.

●

Reports & exports

Reports you can defend. Exports that match. Totals that reconcile between summary and detail views.

●

Subscription & online

Sign in with your RootRecord account for subscription and entitlement; your signed-in workspace is stored with our hosted API when you use the Android app online.
