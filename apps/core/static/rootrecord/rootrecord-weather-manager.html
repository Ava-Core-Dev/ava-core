Products / Weather Manager

Root Record Weather Manager.

Weather awareness on Android (Google Play) and the web at weather.rootrecord.info — alerts, earthquakes, and hazard context in one place instead of juggling a dozen browser tabs.

Web — open in any browser

Use the full Weather Manager from any desktop, laptop, tablet, or phone browser at weather.rootrecord.info. Same RootRecord account as the Android app; saved locations, prefs, and alerts sync between the two.

Open weather.rootrecord.info

No install required — sign in with your RootRecord account.

Android — Google Play

Root Record Weather Manager is public on Google Play. Open the listing on your Android phone or tablet, tap Install, and sign in inside the app with your RootRecord account. Updates arrive through the Play Store like any other app.

Download on Google Play

The running build is shown under About in the app.

APK releases (optional)

Signed APKs and changelogs are also published on GitHub Releases if you prefer to install a build directly.

Latest Android release All releases

Source and public notes: rootrecord-weather-manager-mobile on GitHub.

What it covers

Clarity, without the tab overload.

●

Weather dashboard

Alerts, radar and satellite layers, and forecast periods for saved locations — in a single calm layout.

●

Earthquakes & tsunami

Public-source bulletins for earthquakes and tsunami watches — surfaced when they matter.

●

Cyclone & wildfire

Storm tracks and wildfire feeds from public sources — context for the places on your list.

●

Critical popups

High-severity weather and USGS events near you surface as popups (where included with your plan).

●

RootRecord account

Sign in for subscription and online features, aligned with other RootRecord apps — optional.

●

Google Play download

Install from the public Google Play listing — no closed-test opt-in required.

●

On your device

Your locations and working data stay on the Android device where you run the app. Nothing leaves unless you enable it.

Roots

Roots are tracked on your rootrecord.info account. Balance, game links, buying options, and Discord transfers (“/bal” and “/send” for linked members) are on the website — see Root Units (and your signed-in Account summary). The Android app no longer includes a dedicated Rewards tab; open Settings in the app for a link there.
