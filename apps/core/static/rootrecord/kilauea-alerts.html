Products / Kilauea Alerts

Kilauea Alerts.

Independent companion for Hawaiʻi Island and Kīlauea — dashboards, earthquakes, weather, live official feeds, USGS volcano notices, and NWS alerts in one place. Available now on Android through Google Play, with a members-only web dashboard at kilauea.cloud.

Android — live on Google Play

Kilauea Alerts is live and available to everyone on Google Play. Install it on the Android device where you want volcano notices, earthquake context, weather, live feeds, and Kīlauea-specific summaries.

Download on Google Play

The running build is shown under About in the app.

Featured Content

Lava Watchers

Featured content provider in Kilauea Alerts — independent Kīlauea livestream and commentary alongside official USGS feeds. Open the app’s Live feeds tab or watch on YouTube.

Watch on YouTube

Web dashboard — members only

The desktop web dashboard at kilauea.cloud is a paid members feature for signed-in RootRecord accounts. Use it when you want the larger-screen dashboard experience with member access.

Open members web dashboard

For free public access, use the Android app on Google Play.

What it covers

Official-sourced context, calmer layout.

●

Home & status

Kīlauea-focused dashboard and quick reads so you are not piecing together half a dozen sites.

●

Earthquakes & weather

Recent earthquakes, island-area forecasts, saved locations, and optional GPS for detail screens.

●

Live feeds

Official cameras and streams open in-app so you can watch without juggling bookmarks.

●

USGS & NWS alerts

Volcano notices and weather alerts surfaced together with refresh and notification options where enabled.

●

On your device

Caches and preferences stay on your phone. USGS and NWS are queried from the device; optional weather dashboard summaries may use RootRecord servers with an anonymous guest identifier.

●

Independent app

Not produced or endorsed by the USGS or any government agency. Data comes from public official sources; in any emergency, follow civil defense and official instructions.
