Android beta & dev links.

One maintained page for RootRecord testers: Google Play closed testing, public Play apps, web apps, and builds in active development. Last updated: 2026-06-03

Step 1 — Join the tester group

Closed testing on Google Play requires membership in RootRecord Testing on Google Groups. Use the same Google account on your phone’s Play Store.

How to join the group Open group

Step 2 — Install from Google Play (closed testing)

  1. Join the group (above) and wait until membership is active.
  2. On your Android device, open a Join closed testing link below for the app you want.
  3. Sign in with the same Google account as the Play Store.
  4. Accept the test, then open the Play Store and install or update the app.
  5. Sign in inside the app with your rootrecord.info account where the product supports it.
