About

Capable tools, without giving up control.

RootRecord builds multi-device software for people who want real operational tools — on the desk, in the field, and everywhere between.

Product-specific features, install paths, and screenshots live on each program's page under Products.

How we think about data

Where a product stores a database on your device, that data stays there unless you move it or use features that copy or sync data you have configured. Sign-in and online services are for subscription, sync, backup, or other features when you use them.

Web account

rootrecord.info hosts sign-in, account settings, and billing flows that tie into our apps where configured. Use Account and Billing from your browser to check status and manage your plan.

Custom app development

Need something built for your workflow, team, or customers? Submit an app build request with purpose, platforms, scope, timeline, and how to reach you — we will follow up with next steps.
