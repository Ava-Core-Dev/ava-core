Root Goals.

Turn “I want to do this” into a clear plan you can follow — with your own notes, costs, and progress in one place. Root Goals 1.0.0 · Release 0 is the foundation build.

What we’re building.

●

Guest-first onboarding

Describe your goal before signing up. Drafts persist so you can finish later.

●

Grok AI plan

After sign-in, Grok builds a detailed plan while the UI shows Generating Goal Plan.

●

Actions & Suggestions

AI-generated next steps and ideas you can delete. Always labeled as AI-generated.

●

Progress tracking

Achievements, notes, costs, and income entries while you work the goal.

●

Public sharing

Optional public goals at your custodial address on rootrecord.info and goals.rootrecord.info.

●

Disclaimer

Root Record does not provide financial advice. Actions and Suggestions are AI-generated — use your own judgment.

Tier limits (planned)

Free: 3 active goals. Members: 42. AI refresh limits apply per goal and per day.
