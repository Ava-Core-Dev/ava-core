Visiting Hawaiʻi.

A beautiful, offline-first travel guide for visitors who want authentic local Hawaiʻi — not overcrowded tourist traps. Explore six islands with respect for culture, land, and community.

Join the waitlist Back to home

Travel pono

Hawaiʻi is a living culture and homeland — not a backdrop. Visiting Hawaiʻi emphasizes kapu (sacred restrictions), mālama ʻāina (care for the land), local businesses, hidden gems, and sustainable choices. We avoid promoting overtourism hotspots and chain restaurants unless they have genuine local ties.

This app is developed independently by RootRecord. It is not affiliated with the State of Hawaiʻi or any tourism bureau. In emergencies, always follow civil defense and official instructions.

Six visitor islands

One guide, island by island.

Choose your island once — content preloads for offline use. Switch anytime from the app header.

Oʻahu

The Gathering Place

Urban energy meets legendary surf — North Shore, Kailua, Pearl Harbor context, and local poke counters.

Maui

The Valley Isle

Road to Hāna, Haleakalā sunrise, ʻĪao Valley history, and calm northwest snorkel bays.

Kauaʻi

The Garden Isle

Waimea Canyon, Nā Pali trails, Hanalei Bay, and south-shore local food stops.

Hawaiʻi

Big Island

Volcanoes National Park, black sand beaches, Maunakea stargazing context, and Kona-side snorkel entries.

Molokaʻi

The Friendly Isle

Slow rhythms, guided valley hikes, Kalaupapa history with respect, and famous night bread.

Lānaʻi

The Pineapple Isle

Quiet shores, 4WD adventures, marine preserves, and ferry logistics from Maui.

FAQ

Common questions.

When does it launch?

We are in active development. Waitlist members hear about private betas and public release first. There is no fixed date yet.

Is it free?

The core guide is planned as free with bundled island content. Future premium tiers may add advanced offline maps or exclusive local content — always optional.

How is this different from Kīlauea Alerts?

Kīlauea Alerts focuses on Hawaiʻi Island volcano, earthquake, and hazard context. Visiting Hawaiʻi is a broader travel guide across all major visitor islands.

Do you use my data for ads?

The guide is designed local-first: favorites and notes stay on your device. Waitlist email is only for product updates unless you opt into broader RootRecord mail separately.

Can locals contribute tips?

Not at launch. We plan a moderated, read-only local tips section later so quality and cultural accuracy stay high.
